# Code of Conduct

Trygg is a technical community for building safe, type-driven UI software.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind, direct, and respectful.
- Assume good intent, but accept correction when impact differs from intent.
- Keep feedback focused on ideas, code, and behavior.
- Help make discussion useful for people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Hate speech or exclusionary language.
- Sexualized content or unwanted sexual attention.
- Spam, trolling, or repeated off-topic posting.
- Publishing private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, warn members, mute members, or ban members to protect the community.

Report problems by contacting a maintainer on Discord or opening a private security-style report path if available.
